# IMED Placement OS: Internal College Pricing Strategy

**Goal:** Reduce infrastructure cost to nearly ₹0 to allow for a ₹25,000/year contract where the entire amount becomes your pure profit margin.

Since this is your own college and the user base is relatively small (200 students + 2000 alumni), we can aggressively optimize the architecture to run entirely on **Enterprise Free Tiers**. 

---

## The "Zero-Cost" Infrastructure Plan

### 1. Cloud Hosting (Vercel)
- **Plan:** Vercel Hobby Tier (Free)
- **Why it works:** Vercel's free tier allows 100GB of bandwidth and 100,000 edge function executions per month. A batch of 200 students will not even come close to hitting this limit.
- **Cost:** **$0 / year**

### 2. AI Intelligence (Google Gemini API)
- **Plan:** Gemini API Free Tier
- **Why it works:** Google offers a very generous free tier (1,500 requests per day / 1 Million tokens per minute). Since 200 students won't be running thousands of interviews every single day, this limit is massive.
- **Cost:** **$0 / year**

### 3. Database & Auth (Supabase)
- **Plan:** Supabase Free Tier
- **Why it works:** The free tier gives you 500MB of database space (enough for hundreds of thousands of text rows) and 1GB of file storage (enough for 200 compressed resume PDFs). 
- *Note:* Free tier databases pause after 1 week of inactivity, but since students/admins will use it regularly, it will stay active. If you ever need to upgrade for stability, it is $25/month.
- **Cost:** **$0 / year**

### 4. Domain Name
- **Plan:** Standard Domain Registration
- **Cost:** **$12 / year (₹1,000 / year)**

## Your New Profit Margin

| Item | Amount |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Total Revenue from College** | ₹25,000 / year |
| **Actual Infrastructure Cost** | ~₹1,000 / year |
| **Your Pure Profit / Margin** | **₹24,000 / year** |

### How to Pitch This:
You can charge the college ₹25,000/year under the banner of **"Annual Server Maintenance, AI Licensing, and Tech Support."** Because you are smartly leveraging the free tiers of world-class infrastructure providers, 96% of what the college pays you goes directly into your pocket as margin. 

When you scale this to *other* colleges later with 2,000+ active students each, you can charge them ₹1,50,000/year and switch to the Pro plans we discussed earlier!
